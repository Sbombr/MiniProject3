# Validation Reflection — sbombry1

Across the ten projects validated from gelkhoda, agreement between 
author and validator was extremely high. Most of the fields had levels of detials 
that I was not used to and had no issues with agreeing with. The slight times of
disagreements are mostly nitpicks with things in fields such as CurrentThemes.
Otherwise, there were basically no disagreements between us and there was
a clear understanding between the projects.

The projects each presented qualities of inactivity gaps and regular, consistent
updates. Most gaps were explained from a wide array of development issues such as
releases, implementations with new packages or systems, and general maitenence. More
importantly, none of these gaps meant any projects faced complete abandonment and death.
Each of them are extremely active and seem to remain so in the future.

Some of the main reasons for our disagreements involved a misunderstanding on my part
for reading the activity graphs and assigning the proper description. I overthought
what classifies a graph as stable or declining and it created some confusion. The other
issue was that I had not investigated some of the commits very in-depth, and my 
limited technical knowledge made my explanations vague and prone to disagreements.
Lucky, our discussion solved some of these misconceptions quickly and led to a better
understanding of each of the projets. This exercise shows just how easy it can be to 
find misundestanding between people based on how they interpet the instructions. By 
discussing and agreeing on a level of quality and format, our end data and conclusions 
become more reliable.
